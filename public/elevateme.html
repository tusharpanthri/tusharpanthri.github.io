    How this list was built

    • Sources: 16 LinkedIn searches for entry and associate level roles in the past 72 hours (3,199 listings), the public job boards of about 2,100 companies on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, the Simplify and Jobright new grad trackers, and the career sites of JPMorganChase, American Express, Target, BlackRock, Capital One, Salesforce, Ameriprise, Fidelity, S&P Global, NVIDIA, TikTok and ByteDance.
    • Every remaining posting was read in full and checked sentence by sentence for sponsorship, OPT, CPT, F-1, H-1B, citizenship, U.S. person, green card, clearance, ITAR and export control language. Anything that ruled out visa holders came off the list.
    • Defense, federal and cleared contractors came off, and so did staffing agencies, job aggregators and gig work.
    • Experience: kept when the stated minimum is 3 years or less, or when no minimum is stated. The column shows what the posting says.
    • New this week means first posted Oct 3 to Oct 6. Re-listed means an older opening that was reposted or refreshed in that window. Applicant counts come from LinkedIn as of Oct 6.

    What these checks cannot tell you

    • Silence is not a promise. A posting that says nothing about sponsorship can still ask about it in the application form and filter on the answer.
    • For the STEM OPT extension the employer must be enrolled in E-Verify. Check that before you accept an offer.
    • The big graduate programs at TikTok and ByteDance cap you at 2 applications across both companies.
