# LinkedIn outreach prompts

Connection notes target 300 characters or fewer. Replace the placeholders, then check the final length—long names and company names add characters. Send the resume follow-up after connecting.

## Details to fill in

- Name: [Name]
- Company: [Company]
- Target location: [City/Region]
- SDE resume: [SDE resume link]
- Data Science / AI/ML resume: [DS/AI/ML resume link]
- Specific opening, if available: [Job title or job link]
